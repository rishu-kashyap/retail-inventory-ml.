Model Architecture & Leakage Prevention

1. Leakage-Safe Feature Engineering: In retail forecasting, rolling statistics must strictly be calculated with a shift ≥ 28 days to prevent future sales from leaking into the forecast origin.

2. Tweedie Loss Optimization: Retail SKU demand has non-negative count distribution with excess exact zeros (intermittent demand). Tweedie variance power (1.2) accurately models Poisson frequency with Gamma severity.

3. Operational Integration: Rather than stopping at RMSE, forecast uncertainty (σ) is piped directly into safety stock formulas: SS = z × σd × √L, bridging machine learning directly into operations research.
